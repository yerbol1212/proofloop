# Three-minute ProofLoop demo

Use the [public replay walkthrough](PUBLIC-REPLAY.md) from an isolated offline workbench. It needs no key, private recordings or new inference. Keep the caption **“Cached Meta measurements. Exposed development recordings. Policy replay; no video pixels or physical execution.”** visible. This script demonstrates the evidence-control contribution and its actual limitations.

## 0:00–0:25 — State one concrete developer problem

**Screen:** Video lab → Public tracking replay, contact highlight selected.

**Narration:** “When a visual workflow loses reliable evidence, a plausible track must not silently become proof that a preparation step is complete. ProofLoop keeps supported, unknown and incomplete outcomes separate, binds decisions to source evidence and makes the reason inspectable. Spark supplies semantic assessments; SAM supplies object grounding and tracking. This screen replays cached measurements from real Meta experiments. It does not run a new model or control a robot.”

## 0:25–0:55 — Show uncertainty without inventing a cause

**Screen:** **Contact candidate withheld**, `remote_new:59`. Show the warning and all three method cards.

**Narration:** “This near-gripper proposal is a reason to withhold support, not proof of physical contact. Current-view confirmation is already UNKNOWN here, so the warning did not uniquely prevent acceptance. These method decisions are recomputed from cached inputs. The authored scoring label is displayed separately and never goes into the policy.”

## 0:55–1:30 — Expose the cost of the conservative rule

**Screen:** **Clear target unnecessarily withheld**, `screwdriver_new:5`, then episode-2 table.

**Narration:** “This clear target is supported by current-view confirmation. Adding the warning rejects it because a cached gripper proposal lies three source pixels away. Its reported confidence is about 0.544; that is not a calibrated probability of correctness. The cost is visible: clear retention falls from four out of six to three out of six. Both variants support zero of ten unclear references on this exposed cohort. We preserve the failure instead of presenting only the favorable cases.”

## 1:30–2:00 — Show incomplete assessment and current-ending proof

**Screen:** **Incomplete assessment stays unscored**, `remote_3:5`; then select `remote_new:5` and expand current-view confirmation proof.

**Narration:** “The original incomplete network outcome stays unscored and remains in the seven-positive denominator. It cannot be converted into a successful prediction. This supported checkpoint, by contrast, has distinct source-bound confirmation receipts ending at the current view with at least 500 milliseconds between them. The hashes check consistency; they do not independently authenticate the camera or API.”

## 2:00–2:30 — Connect the diagnostic to the live product boundary

**Screen:** Inspection or Task, then Workflow. Do not start a paid inspection for the recording. If historical or absent evidence blocks preparation, leave it blocked.

**Narration:** “The separate live placement workbench turns reviewed requirements into pass, fail or unknown and requests specific missing evidence. It proposes a bounded correction but needs new observations before changing the result. Historical tracking replay cannot unlock this preparation gate. A current operator following the displayed correction and completing the full physical loop is still unvalidated. Guided Spark plus SAM has also not shown an accuracy advantage over Spark alone.”

## 2:30–3:00 — Show reproducibility and identify contributions

**Screen:** Public repository, harsh review, then saved doctor/replay output or the local commands.

**Narration:** “During the hackathon we built the checker, Meta adapters, evidence requests, repair planner, review and recovery boundaries, durable jobs, shared request accounting, replay inspector and installable workbench. The public replay works without a key or private recordings. New offline inference is rejected before admission; lost responses can recover the original job. Muse Spark and SAM are pretrained Meta models. ALOHA and LeRobot supplied external recordings. Our next evidence must come from new source families, independent users and a current physical correction.”
